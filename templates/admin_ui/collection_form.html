A short code that starts every ARK in this collection, right after the NAAN: utsc in ark:/61220/utsc1. It keeps this collection's ARKs apart from your other collections' ARKs. It must be unique: it may not be the same as, start with, or be the beginning of a prefix already used under the same NAAN. If utsc exists, utsc1 and ut are refused, because the two collections could end up giving out the same ARK. Hyphens are not allowed. Ending it with a digit, such as b3, is the ARK community's custom. {% if errors.prefix %}{{ errors.prefix }}{% endif %}
A number that identifies your organisation in the ARK world. Every ARK your organisation creates starts with it: 61220 in ark:/61220/utsc1. The name of your organisation, the one the NAAN stands for, written the way you want people to see it, e.g. University of Toronto Scarborough Library. Visitors see it when they open ark:/<NAAN>/ without an item after it. If several collections share this NAAN, visitors see the one from the collection whose name comes first alphabetically, so give them all the same text.
